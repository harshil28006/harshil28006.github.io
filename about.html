01 / Introduction

Who I Am

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at the National Institute of Technology, Durgapur (NIT Durgapur), currently in my second year (2024–2028). I maintain a CGPA of 8.22/10 and my academic focus bridges mechanical engineering fundamentals with applied robotics, autonomous systems, and data engineering.

My technical journey started with a fascination for autonomous systems — how machines can sense, plan, and act in the real world. This led me to dive into ROS 2, Gazebo simulation, and ArduPilot for marine robotics, and separately into cloud data engineering with Snowflake and DBT. I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware control and software intelligence.

Outside of technical work, I am an active Training & Placement Coordinator for the Mechanical Engineering department and a Senior Coordinator at SPIC MACAY, where I organize cultural events that celebrate classical Indian arts.
